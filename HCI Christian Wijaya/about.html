About Christian Wijaya

A blend of heritage and innovation in every fashion creation

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Christian Wijaya was born out of a vision to create luxurious fashion that is not only beautiful but also meaningful. Starting from a small atelier in Jakarta, our brand has grown into one of the leading names in the Indonesian fashion industry.

Each of our collections reflects our dedication to excellence, combining traditional techniques with contemporary designs to create timeless yet relevant pieces.

Christian Wijaya

Founder & Creative Director

"Fashion is not just about clothes, it's about telling stories, expressing identity, and creating positive change."

With over 15 years of experience in the fashion industry, Christian Wijaya has dedicated his life to creating inspiring works. A graduate of Parsons School of Design, New York, Christian brings a global perspective while remaining rooted in Indonesian cultural heritage.
